About

Hi, I'm Alok. I write about things that interest me — technology, ideas, the way people work and think.

I started this site because I wanted a place to think out loud. Not a newsletter, not a brand — just a place to put words and see what comes of it.

I believe writing is a tool for thinking, not just communicating. The best essays are ones where the author is figuring something out in real time, and takes you along for the ride.

If something here resonates, I'd love to hear from you.
